# ARES — Three-minute presentation script

**0:00–0:20 | Mission framing**
“ARES is a planetary exploration decision-support prototype. The question is not simply where Mars is interesting — it is how we turn observations into better exploration decisions.”

**0:20–0:55 | Mars Explorer**
“Here is Jezero Crater, the Perseverance landing region. We can switch between NASA's global Viking mosaic and MOLA shaded relief, inspect coordinates, and explore mission context. Notice that ARES does not invent a local elevation value: until we sample a MOLA raster cell, it says not sampled.”

**0:55–1:30 | Route Planner**
“Let's plan from the verified landing-site reference toward a notional delta approach. ARES compares a compact route with a longer science detour. Distances are calculated using great-circle geometry on Mars. These lines are conceptual, not rover navigation or certified safe paths.”

**1:30–1:55 | Hazard Intelligence**
“The hazard panel makes uncertainty visible. Steep slopes and rock abundance need local high-resolution data; dust and solar availability need environmental inputs. The relative score is a transparent educational heuristic, not a scientific risk product.”

**1:55–2:40 | Commander Mode**
“Now I am the mission commander. A science stop consumes scenario power, water, oxygen, and time. A systems check conserves mobility but still consumes crew resources. The event stream records every decision, and the debrief explains the outcome.”

**2:40–3:00 | Scientific transparency**
“ARES separates observed NASA source material, derived estimates, and simulated values. The next engineering step is to ingest and sample local MOLA/HiRISE terrain products and add obstacle-aware path costs. This is a working educational prototype — not a flight or crew-operations tool.”
